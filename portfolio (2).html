About Me

I'm a hardware designer and PCB specialist with a passion for creating precision electronics. My journey in embedded systems has taken me from robotics to wearable technology, always with a focus on quality and technical excellence.

Currently at INSAT (Institut National des Sciences Appliquées et de Technologie), I combine academic rigor with practical engineering. My work spans motor control systems, IoT platforms, and custom wearables—each project a testament to meticulous design and robust implementation.

I believe in open-source hardware and knowledge sharing. All my projects are documented and available on GitHub, reflecting my commitment to transparency and community contribution.
